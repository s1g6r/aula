# Demo video script (90 seconds, captions only)

A screen recording with on-screen captions. No voice and no camera. Almost all of it is recorded from the Demo Replay (aulaapp.xyz/demo), so it needs no second person or phone. Optional music: something quiet and royalty-free, low under the captions.

## Before recording

- Chrome, window about 1440 x 900, zoom 100%, bookmarks bar hidden, no other tabs showing.
- Open https://aulaapp.xyz/demo once to wake the server, then reload so the replay starts from 0:00.
- Record with macOS Screenshot (Cmd+Shift+5, "Record Selected Portion"). Record each scene as its own clip; they're easier to trim.
- Add the captions in the editor (iMovie titles, CapCut text, or similar): large, high-contrast, bottom or top center, on screen long enough to read twice.

## The script

| Time | On screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 to 0:06** | Landing page, top. Slowly scroll to the three statistics. | **5.3 million US students are learning English.** |
| **0:06 to 0:12** | Hold on the "June 30, 2026" statistic. | **The free tool their teachers used to caption lessons was retired this summer.** |
| **0:12 to 0:20** | Click "Watch a live demo". The replay starts: the teacher's screen on the left, a student's phone on the right, the first English lines appearing. | **Aula: the teacher just talks. Every student reads along on their own phone.** |
| **0:20 to 0:28** | Spanish lines appear on the phone a moment after the English. | **...in their own language, live. Here: Spanish.** |
| **0:28 to 0:35** | Tap "العربية" above the phone. The captions switch to Arabic and read right to left. | **One tap for Arabic. Right to left. 13 languages.** |
| **0:35 to 0:41** | Switch back to "Español". Tap a highlighted word on the phone (for example "fotosíntesis"). The definition sheet opens. | **Key words are highlighted. Tap one: a simple definition in your language...** |
| **0:41 to 0:48** | Tap "Escucharlo" (hear it). Show "Guardado en Mis palabras". | **...and how to say it in English. Translation is a bridge, not a crutch.** |
| **0:48 to 0:55** | Click "Jump to the lost moment". The phone's "I'm lost" button pulses; the teacher's panel shows "3 students lost at: ...". | **Lost? One silent tap. The teacher sees how many, and where. Never who.** |
| **0:55 to 1:02** | The teacher's Questions panel shows the Spanish question translated into English. | **Ask in your own language. The teacher reads it in English and answers.** |
| **1:02 to 1:10** | Drag the replay bar near the end. The phone shows "Read the recap"; open it and switch the phone to "Tiếng Việt". | **When class ends: a recap in every student's language.** |
| **1:10 to 1:18** | Scroll the recap: summary, key words, check-yourself questions. | **Absent? The same page is a "What you missed" link, in any language.** |
| **1:18 to 1:24** | Open aulaapp.xyz, click "Try it live", start a lesson, show the QR code and join code. (Optional: cut to projector mode.) | **Free. No app, no student accounts. Scan and follow along.** |
| **1:24 to 1:30** | Back to the landing page hero with the Aula wordmark. | **Aula. Every lesson, understood. aulaapp.xyz** |
